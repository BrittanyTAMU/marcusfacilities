Questions about outplacement services

Do you offer outplacement services for companies?

Yes.

Marcus provides outplacement and reemployment support for companies dealing with layoffs, WARN notices, reductions in force, closures, and other workforce changes.

Your company sponsors the service, and affected employees receive job-search help.

What is outplacement?

Outplacement is help a company provides to employees who are losing their jobs.

Instead of saying:

“Sorry about the layoff. Here is your final paycheck. Good luck.”

the company gives employees support finding their next opportunity.

Marcus focuses on the actual job search: finding jobs, preparing applications, reaching employers, and keeping the search moving.

Is Marcus a reverse recruiting company?

For an individual job seeker, you may hear the term reverse recruiting.

A normal recruiter works for the company trying to hire someone.

A reverse recruiter works for the person trying to get hired.

When a company pays for that help for employees affected by a layoff, it is usually called outplacement or reemployment support.

Different name. Same basic goal: help someone find their next job.

Do you help employees after a WARN notice?

Yes.

Companies facing a WARN notice or planned mass layoff can sponsor Marcus for affected employees.

Starting the job search before someone's final day can give them more time to find their next opportunity.

Can employees start looking before their layoff date?

Yes.

If the company has already told employees their positions are ending, they may be able to begin their job searches before their last day.

Marcus can help them start looking instead of waiting until the paycheck stops.

Do you charge a placement fee when someone gets hired?

No.

Marcus does not charge the employee's next employer a recruiting or placement fee.

The sponsoring company pays Marcus for the outplacement service.

If an employee gets hired somewhere else, congratulations to them. There is no surprise bill waiting for the new employer.

Does Marcus guarantee everyone will find a job?

No.

No outplacement company can control an employer's hiring decision.

What Marcus can do is give employees tools and support to find opportunities, prepare applications, reach the right people, follow up, and keep moving.

We help with the search.

The next employer makes the hiring decision.

Can Marcus support a large layoff?

Potentially, yes.

The size of the program, number of affected employees, level of support, timeline, and reporting needs can be discussed with Marcus before the program begins.

Can we sponsor only some employees?

Potentially, yes.

A company may want different levels of support based on the situation, employee group, location, or program.

We can discuss what makes sense for your workforce.
