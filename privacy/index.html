Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Who we are

Marcus Reemployment is operated by Marcus Facilities LLC. This policy covers www.marcusfacilities.com and the platform at app.marcusfacilities.com. Questions: sales@marcusfacilities.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, including:

  • Name, email address, and account password, stored by our sign-in provider
  • Career information, resume files, job-search preferences, and application answers you enter
  • Messages you ask the platform to draft or send
  • Payment details processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers

3. Google account and Gmail

If you connect Gmail, Marcus Reemployment requests permission to send email from that mailbox and to see the email address of the connected account. We use that permission only to send messages you or your assigned Marcus worker choose to send for your job search. We do not read your inbox, use Gmail data for advertising, or sell Gmail data. You can disconnect Gmail from your Marcus account at any time.

4. Artificial intelligence and other providers

The platform uses third-party tools to do parts of the job search. When you ask for a draft, a resume review, application help, or contact research, the text you submit for that task can be sent to those providers. Review anything they produce before you use it on an application or in an email.

  • OpenAI (GPT). Used to draft application answers, outreach, and job-search research from the career information you provide for that request. Protected demographic details are not placed in the Chrome helper. Do not type passwords into a GPT question.
  • Stripe. Processes subscriptions and one-time payments. Stripe handles the card number.
  • Google. Sends the email you or your worker choose to send from the Gmail account you connect, and returns that account’s email address so Marcus can show the From address.
  • Job search. Your saved role and location are sent to a job-listing search provider so Marcus can return individual postings.
  • Professional email lookup. A Target Company domain and a professional name may be sent to an email-finding provider to locate a work address. Marcus shows an address only after it passes the platform’s checks.
  • Account email and hosting. Sign-in, case records, and resume files are stored with our database host. Invite and account email may be sent by an email delivery provider.

These providers process data to perform the service you asked for. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use Gmail contents for advertising.

5. How we use information

  • Provide the job-search platform, resume help, and customer support
  • Send the email you or your assigned worker ask the platform to send
  • Process subscriptions and one-time payments
