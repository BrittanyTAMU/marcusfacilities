Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Who we are

Marcus Reemployment is operated by Marcus Facilities LLC. This policy covers www.marcusfacilities.com and the platform at app.marcusfacilities.com. Questions: sales@marcusfacilities.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, including:

  • Name and email address
  • Account password, stored by our sign-in provider
  • Career information, resume files, and job-search preferences you enter
  • Payment details processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers

3. Google account and Gmail

If you connect Gmail, Marcus Reemployment requests permission to send email from that mailbox (the Gmail send permission) and to see the email address of the connected account. We use that permission only to send messages you or your assigned Marcus worker choose to send for your job search. We do not read your inbox, use Gmail data for advertising, or sell Gmail data. You can disconnect Gmail from your Marcus account at any time.

4. How we use information

  • Provide the job-search platform and customer support
  • Send the email you ask the platform to send
  • Process subscriptions and one-time payments

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
