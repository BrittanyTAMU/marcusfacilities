Questions about finding a second job

Can you help me find a second job?

Yes.

You do not have to be unemployed to use Marcus.

Tell us what kind of job you want and where you can work. Marcus can help you search while you keep the job you already have.

I need more money. Can Marcus help me find extra work?

Yes.

Marcus can help you look for another job, a better-paying job, or work that gives you another source of income.

We cannot promise a certain salary, schedule, interview, or job offer. But we can help you find opportunities and keep applying.

Can someone help me apply for jobs because I don't have time?

Yes. That is one of the main reasons Marcus exists.

You already have a job, family, school, errands, sleep and hopefully some kind of life.

Marcus can help with job searches, applications, company contacts, and follow-up so you do not have to spend every free hour doing it yourself.

Do I have to quit my current job first?

No.

Please keep your paycheck.

You can search, apply, and interview for other jobs while you are still working.

If you find something better, then you can decide what you want to do next.

Can I use Marcus to find a better-paying job instead of a second job?

Absolutely.

Maybe you do not actually want two jobs.

Maybe you just need one job that pays enough.

Marcus can help you search for a better-paying opportunity while you continue working at your current job.

Can Marcus find part-time or weekend jobs?

Marcus can search based on the type of work and location you choose. If you are looking for part-time, evening, weekend, remote, or other flexible work, make that part of your search.

Job schedules and availability depend on what employers are hiring for at the time.

Will my current employer know I am searching?

Marcus does not contact your current employer just because you start a job search.

You decide which jobs you want to pursue and who you want to contact.

As with any private job search, be careful about what you post publicly and who you tell.

Can Marcus guarantee I will make more money?

No.

We would love that superpower, but employers still control salaries and hiring decisions.

Marcus can help you find opportunities that match what you are looking for, prepare stronger applications, and keep your search moving.

Your free time should still be free sometimes.

You already have a job.

Your job search does not need to take over every night and weekend too.

Keep working. Keep earning. Let us help you look for what’s next.
