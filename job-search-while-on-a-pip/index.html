Questions about being on a PIP

Should I look for a job while on a PIP?

It may be a smart idea.

A PIP does not always lead to getting fired, but it does mean you should think about your next step.

You can keep doing your current job while quietly looking at other options. If everything works out at your current company, great. If it does not, you already started your search.

What should I do after getting put on a PIP?

First, read the PIP carefully and understand what your employer is asking you to improve.

Keep copies of important documents and emails. If you have questions about your legal rights, talk to an employment lawyer.

Then think about your backup plan.

If you would rather not wait to see what happens, Marcus can help you start looking for your next job now.

Can I get fired after a PIP?

Yes. Some people are fired after a PIP.

Other people complete the PIP and keep their jobs.

Marcus cannot tell you what your employer will decide. What we can do is help you prepare so that losing your job is not also the day you have to start your job search from zero.

I’m on a PIP. Should I start looking for another job?

If you want another option, yes.

Looking does not mean you have to leave.

You can apply, interview, and see what is out there while you are still employed. You might even find something better.

Will my employer know I am looking for another job?

Marcus does not contact your current employer just because you start a search.

You choose the jobs you want to pursue and the people you want to contact.

Of course, no job search can promise complete secrecy, so be careful about what you post publicly and who you tell.

Can Marcus help me apply while I am still working?

Yes.

Marcus is built for people who do not have hours every night to spend searching and applying.

We can help with job searches, applications, company contacts, outreach, and follow-up so you can keep working while also preparing for what comes next.

What if I survive the PIP?

Then you have options.

You can stay where you are, stop your search, or keep looking if you found something better.

Starting a job search does not mean you have decided to leave. It simply means you are not putting your entire future in someone else's hands.

You do not have to wait until you lose your job.

If you are on a PIP, worried about getting fired, unhappy at work, or just want a backup plan, you can start looking now.

Keep the paycheck. Start the search. See what else is out there.
