What is a WARN notice?

A WARN notice is connected to certain large layoffs or business closings.

For Marcus, the important part is simple:

People are about to lose their jobs, and they may need help finding the next one.

Marcus is not a government agency and we do not provide legal advice about WARN laws.

We provide the job-search support that can come after the notice.

Your company can pay for the support.

Companies can sponsor Marcus for employees affected by a layoff.

The employee gets their own account and job-search support.

Your company pays for the service.

There is no placement fee charged to the employee's next employer when they get hired.

You are paying to help people get back to work.

Workforce programs can sponsor workers too.

Marcus can also work with workforce programs, nonprofits, community organizations, and other groups helping people after layoffs.

A company and a workforce partner may also be able to work together to support affected employees.

The goal is simple:

Help people spend less time figuring out how to job search and more time actually job searching.

Questions about WARN notices and layoffs

Do you help people after a WARN notice or mass layoff?

Yes.

Marcus can help affected employees find jobs, prepare applications, contact recruiters and hiring managers, and follow up.

A company, workforce program, nonprofit, or other organization may sponsor the service for affected workers.

Do you offer outplacement after a reduction in force?

Yes.

Marcus can provide outplacement and reemployment support after:

WARN notices, mass layoffs, reductions in force (RIFs), department closures, office or plant closings, restructuring, and position cuts.

Different words. Same problem: people need help finding what comes next.

Can employees start their job search before their last day?

Yes.

If employees already know their jobs are ending, they do not necessarily have to wait until their final day to start looking.

Starting earlier gives them more time to find opportunities, apply, interview, and prepare for the next step.

What does Marcus actually do for laid-off employees?

Depending on the program, Marcus can help employees:

Search for jobs, improve resumes, prepare applications, fill out applications faster, find company contacts, write outreach, follow up after applying, and stay organized during the search.

We do not just give someone a list of job websites and say:

“Good luck. You've got this.”

We help with the actual work.

Is Marcus a government Rapid Response program?

No.

Marcus is a private job-search and reemployment service.

We can work alongside employers, workforce programs, nonprofits, and other organizations, but we are not a government Rapid Response agency.

Does Marcus guarantee employees will get another job?

No.

We cannot control who an employer decides to hire.

What we can do is help affected workers find opportunities, submit stronger applications, reach the right people, follow up, and keep their searches moving.

Does the employee have to pay?

If the company or another organization fully sponsors the employee, the sponsor pays Marcus for the covered service.

The employee receives the job-search support without paying for that sponsored service themselves.

Can Marcus help with a large group of laid-off employees?

Potentially, yes.

Tell us how many employees are affected, where they are located, when the layoff is happening, and what level of help you want to provide.

We can talk through what makes sense for your group.
