## CORE COMPETENCIES

Capabilities are organized into four pillars. Services are delivered with documentation and compliance support suitable for government and prime contractor requirements.

### FACILITIES OPERATIONS

- **Janitorial & Sanitation** — Daily custodial and high-traffic floor care; contract-spec aligned.
- **Furniture Assembly** — Rapid deployment and installation for office, school, and facility expansions.
- **Snow & Ice Management** — De-icing and plowing for facility access; 24/7 availability for critical weather events.
- **Commercial Pressure Washing** — Building exterior and equipment restoration; environmentally appropriate methods.
- **Signage & Handyman** — Compliance-ready sign installation and minor trade repairs. *As a facilities support prime, we manage the facility holistically; for plumbing-specific work we coordinate or subcontract licensed plumbers per state requirements (NJ/TX). We do not hold ourselves out as a plumbing-only contractor.*

### LOGISTICS & TRANSPORT

- **USPS Postal Support** — Positioned for HCR and CDS route operations; vehicle and personnel compliance.
- **Courier & Supply Chain** — Time-sensitive document courier and MRO/supply delivery to multi-site locations.
- **Secure Shredding** — Audited chain-of-custody document destruction; certificate of destruction where required.

*Relevant NAICS: 491110, 492110, 561730, 561990.*

### ENVIRONMENTAL & SPECIALIZED SUPPORT

- **Vegetation Management** — Commercial mowing and right-of-way weed control; application and recordkeeping per label and contract.
